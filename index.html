❓ Quick Help

Last updated: 7 Aug 2026 · v155R
🎫 1. Raise a Ticket
Tap the mic and describe the problem out loud, or type it — e.g. “bain marie coil not heating.” The app finds the closest match automatically. You can also browse categories manually if you prefer.
📸 2. Add a Photo
A clear photo helps the attender arrive prepared with the right parts and tools.
📋 3. Track Progress
Open “My Tickets” from the menu to see live status, SLA countdown, and who’s assigned. Tap any ticket for full details.
💬 4. Chat & Confirm
Message the attender directly from the ticket. Once the work is done, confirm resolution to share the closing OTP — the ticket only closes after you confirm.
↻ 5. Not Fixed Yet?
If the issue comes back, reopen the same ticket instead of raising a new one — keeps the full history in one place.
